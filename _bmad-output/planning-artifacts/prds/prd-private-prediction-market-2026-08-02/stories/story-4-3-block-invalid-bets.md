# Story 4.3: Block invalid bet submissions

As the system,
I want to prevent invalid bets,
So that only eligible members can participate in open markets.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Non-members cannot place bets.
- Closed markets cannot accept bets.
- Rejected bets explain why the action failed.
