# Story 1.3: Define shared app-wide states and empty screens

As a user,
I want clear empty, loading, and offline states,
So that I always understand what the app can do next.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Empty and loading states explain what is happening.
- Offline screens distinguish cached read-only content from unavailable actions.
- The app gives the user a clear next step when content is missing.
