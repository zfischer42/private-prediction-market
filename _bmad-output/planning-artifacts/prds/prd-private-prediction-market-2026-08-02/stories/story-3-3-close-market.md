# Story 3.3: Close a market to new bets

As an organizer,
I want a market to stop accepting new bets after closure,
So that the result is fair and predictable.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Closed markets reject new bet submissions.
- The UI clearly shows that the market is closed.
- The closed state is enforced by the system, not only the UI.
