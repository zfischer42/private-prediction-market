# Story 3.2: Edit or retire a draft market

As an organizer,
I want to adjust a draft market before bets are placed,
So that I can correct mistakes without affecting history.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Draft markets can be updated before participation begins.
- Draft markets can be removed before participation begins.
- Participant history is not affected by draft-only edits.
