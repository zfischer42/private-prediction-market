# Story 3.1: Create a market

As an organizer,
I want to create a market with a question and outcomes,
So that members have a clear thing to bet on.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The market is created inside a space.
- The market includes a question and possible outcomes.
- The market is visible to members of the space.
