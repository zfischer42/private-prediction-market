# Story 2.3: Join a space from an invite

As an invited user,
I want to join a space from an invite link,
So that I can participate without manual setup.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- A valid invite joins the correct space.
- An uninvited user cannot join by guessing an identifier.
- The new member appears in the space membership list after acceptance.
