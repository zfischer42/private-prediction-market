# Story 2.2: Invite friends into a space

As an organizer,
I want to generate an invite link or invite flow,
So that friends can join the private space without public discovery.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Invites are scoped to a single space.
- An invited user can join through the invite path.
- The invite flow does not expose unrelated spaces.
