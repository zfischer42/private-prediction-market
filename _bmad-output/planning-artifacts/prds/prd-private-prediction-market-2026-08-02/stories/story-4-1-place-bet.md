# Story 4.1: Place a bet on an open market

As a member,
I want to place a bet on an open market,
So that I can participate in the group prediction.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- A member can select an outcome and stake.
- The bet is recorded when submitted.
- The market reflects the new bet immediately.
