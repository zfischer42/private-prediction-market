# Story 4.2: View my bet history in a space

As a member,
I want to view my bet history,
So that I can remember what I picked and how I performed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The user can see past bets in the selected space.
- Each bet shows current or final status.
- The history reflects resolved outcomes after settlement.
